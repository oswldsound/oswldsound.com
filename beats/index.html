What is the difference between Exclusive and Non-Exclusive licenses?

Exclusive: The beat is removed from the store and no further licenses are issued. The producer royalty share is specified in the license agreement.

Non-Exclusive: You can use the beat in your song, and it remains available to other artists.

Can I release my song on streaming platforms?

Yes. You can release your song on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music, subject to your license terms.

What does the Basic License streams limit mean?

The Basic License includes up to 100,000 total streams across all platforms. Contact me before reaching the limit for a discounted Unlimited or Exclusive License upgrade.

What does "No Content ID" mean?

Non-Exclusive licenses do not allow you to register the beat with any Content ID system. Exclusive License is exempt from this rule.

Do you use uncleared samples?

No. All samples are cleared for commercial use.

Are producer tags removed?

Yes. All files are delivered without producer tags.

When will I receive the files?

Your files are available instantly after purchase on the receipt page and via email.

How can I review the license terms before purchasing?

Click "Add to Cart", proceed to "Checkout", and select "Review License".

Is it possible to get a refund?

Refunds are considered only for missing or incorrect files or duplicate orders, in accordance with BeatStars' terms and applicable law.
