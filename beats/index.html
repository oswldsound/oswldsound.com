Licensing

What does exclusive use mean?
The beat is licensed exclusively to one artist, with no further licenses issued after purchase. You receive full ownership of the sound recording (the Master), while the underlying musical composition remains shared according to the splits outlined in the agreement. The beat is automatically removed from the store.
Can I release my song on streaming platforms?
Yes. You can release your song on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music, subject to the terms of your license.
How are royalties split?
The writer's share is split equally (50/50) between you and me. The publisher's share is split equally (50/50) between you and BeatStars Publishing. I also receive a 3% Producer Royalty on Net Receipts generated from exploitation of the new Masters.
Do you use uncleared samples?
No. I only use royalty-free samples cleared for commercial use.
Can I register my song with Content ID?
Yes. Unlike Non-Exclusive licenses, Exclusive licenses allow you to register your song with Content ID.

Purchasing

How can I purchase a beat and review the license terms?
Click "ADD" on the beat you want, proceed to "Checkout", and select "Review License" to read the terms before completing your purchase.
What files are included?
Your purchase includes untagged WAV and MP3 files, multitrack files, a beat information document, and a license agreement.
How can I receive the files?
Your files are available instantly after purchase on the receipt page and via email.
Is it possible to get a refund?
Refunds are considered only for missing or incorrect files or duplicate orders, in accordance with BeatStars' terms and applicable law.
